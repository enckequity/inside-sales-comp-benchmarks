# Regional Adjustment — National → Pennsylvania → Lancaster

National benchmarks need a regional adjustment for a Pennsylvania (Lancaster / Gap area) employer. The key distinction throughout: **cost-of-LABOR** (what employers actually pay in that market — the right basis for setting comp) vs. **cost-of-LIVING** (what things cost there — *not* the basis for pay).

---

## Two cost-of-labor signals

### A. Occupation-specific (most relevant) — BLS OEWS 41-4012, May 2024

Comparing the *same occupation* (sales reps) across geographies — the cleanest read for this exact role:

| Market | Median total cash | Ratio to US |
|---|---|---|
| US national | $66,780 | 1.00 |
| Pennsylvania | $65,490 | **0.981** |
| Lancaster, PA MSA | $63,060 | **0.944** |

→ For *this occupation*, Lancaster pays ~**0.94×** the national median; PA overall ~0.98×.

### B. All-occupations (broader, runs lower) — BLS OEWS all jobs

Comparing the average of *all* jobs in the metro vs. nation:

| Market | All-occ mean wage | Ratio to US |
|---|---|---|
| US national | $65,470 | 1.00 |
| Pennsylvania | $61,920 | **0.946** |
| Lancaster, PA MSA | $55,560 | **0.849** |

Sources: BLS OEWS May 2023 all-occupations — [Lancaster MSA](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes_29540.htm), [US](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes_nat.htm), [PA](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes_pa.htm). Cross-check: [Lancaster County QCEW 2024](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dli/documents/cwia/products/qcew/qcew%20lancaster%20county.pdf) avg annual wage $59,966 (all industries).

→ Across *all* jobs, Lancaster runs ~0.85× national — but that's dragged down by many hyper-local low-wage jobs. Sales talent competes in a broader regional market, so the occupation-specific 0.94× is the better anchor for *this* role.

---

## Recommended regional multiplier

**Apply ~0.90–0.94× to US-national pay for a Lancaster, PA employer.**

- **Upper bound 0.94×** = the occupation-specific Lancaster-vs-US sales-rep ratio. Best single number for this role.
- **Lower bound ~0.85×** = the raw all-occupations metro ratio. A floor — likely too low for competitive sales pay.
- **Practical pick ~0.90–0.92×:** clearly below national (a real local discount exists) but not so steep you under-pay and lose closers to nearby Harrisburg/Reading/Lancaster competitors.

PA overall sits only ~2–5% under national, so don't over-discount.

---

## Cost-of-LIVING (context only — do NOT set pay from this)

Lancaster's cost of living lands near national parity, and sources disagree by method:

| Source | Index (US = 100) | Date |
|---|---|---|
| AreaVibes | 93 | 2026 |
| BestPlaces | ~99.5 | 2026 |
| ERI | ~103 | 2026 |
| Salary.com | 107 | 2026-05-26 |

Note the mismatch: cost-of-**living** (~93–107, ≈ national) is **higher** than cost-of-**labor** (~0.85–0.94). Lancaster employers pay below-national wages while local costs are near national. Relevant context for recruiting messaging, but **pay is set from cost-of-labor, not cost-of-living.**

## Minimum-wage / floor context

- **Pennsylvania minimum wage = $7.25/hr** (still the federal floor; unchanged 17+ years). [OnPay](https://onpay.com/insights/minimum-wage-by-state-summary/pennsylvania/)
- A bill to raise it ($11 in 2027 → $13 → $15) **passed the PA House in early 2026 but is not law** (Senate hasn't acted). [PA Gov newsroom](https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2026-press-releases/pa-house-passes-bill-to-increase-minimum-wage-following-gov-shap)
- No Lancaster-specific local minimum. Tipped minimum $2.83/hr (not relevant to salaried sales).

Far below any sales base discussed here, so it doesn't bind — but worth knowing the legislative direction if you're setting a multi-year plan.
