# Base Salary & On-Target Earnings (OTE)

How much total cash a full-cycle inside trailer-sales closer earns, and how to split it into base vs. on-target. Three independent evidence streams are reconciled here:

1. **BLS government wage data** — grounded, but reports *total cash* (base + commission bundled together).
2. **General inside-sales salary aggregators** — separate base from commission, not SaaS-skewed.
3. **SaaS/tech "AE" comp surveys** — useful for *structure* only; they **overstate dollar levels ~2–3×** for an equipment dealer and are flagged as such.

---

## 1. The grounded anchor — BLS OEWS, SOC 41-4012

**Occupation:** *Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products* — the official code a trailer/equipment closer maps to (the product isn't technical/scientific, so it's 41-4012, not 41-4011).

**Survey:** BLS Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics, **May 2024** (most recent full release).

**Important caveat:** OEWS wages **include** base pay, commissions, and production bonuses combined. It does **not** separate base from commission. So every figure below is **total annual cash**, and the *base-only* component for a commissioned closer sits **below** these medians. The percentile spread is the useful signal for how much of the pay is variable.

| Market | Employment | Mean | 10th | 25th | **Median** | 75th | 90th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **US national** | 1,266,860 | $81,470 | $37,860 | $49,040 | **$66,780** | $97,570 | $134,470 |
| **Pennsylvania** | 53,290 | $80,780 | $37,330 | $48,980 | **$65,490** | $98,250 | $132,960 |
| **Lancaster, PA MSA** | 2,720 | $81,200 | $38,630 | $48,420 | **$63,060** | $87,520 | $127,790 |

Sources: BLS OEWS May 2024 — [national 41-4012 profile](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/oes414012.htm); state & metro figures from the BLS downloadable data files ([national](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm24nat.zip), [state](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm24st.zip), [metro](https://www.bls.gov/oes/special-requests/oesm24ma.zip)). National median corroborated by the [Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/sales/wholesale-and-manufacturing-sales-representatives.htm) ($66,780).

**Read it as experience tiers:**
- **Entry / ramping (25th pct):** ~$48k–$49k total cash
- **Solid full-cycle closer (median):** ~$63k (Lancaster) – $67k (national)
- **Strong performer (75th pct):** ~$88k (Lancaster) – $98k (national)
- **Top performer (90th pct):** ~$128k (Lancaster) – $134k (national)

## 2. Separating base from commission — non-SaaS aggregators

These sources break out base vs. commission and are **not** tech-skewed, so they're the cleanest read on *base salary* for an inside closer:

| Metric | Figure | Source | Date | SaaS-skewed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inside Sales Rep — **base** | ~$39k (10th) / **~$53k** / ~$73k (90th) | [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/research/US/Job=Inside_Sales_Representative/Salary) | 2026-05-19 | No |
| Inside Sales Rep — **base** | **$51,636** | [Salary.com (ISR I)](https://www.salary.com/research/salary/benchmark/inside-sales-representative-i-salary) | 2026-03-01 | No |
| Inside Sales Rep — **base + commission** | $60.4k base + ~$12k commission | [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career/inside-sales-representative/salaries) | 2026 | No |
| Inside Sales Rep — **total pay** | $37k / ~$60k / $76k | [PayScale](https://www.payscale.com/research/US/Job=Inside_Sales_Representative/Salary) | 2026-05-19 | No |
| Inside Sales Rep — **total comp** | $88.5k (25th) / $108.7k / $136.8k (75th) | [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/inside-sales-representative-salary-SRCH_KO0,27.htm) | 2026 | Partial (mixes tech + general; this is *total*, not base) |

**Takeaway:** non-SaaS base salary clusters at **~$50k–$53k** nationally, with total cash **~$60k–$76k** once commission is layered on — squarely consistent with the BLS national total-cash median of ~$67k.

## 3. SaaS/tech "AE" surveys — the inflated ceiling (use for structure, not dollars)

The same closing motion in software is called an "Account Executive" and pays far more, because software margins (70–90%), recurring contract values, and VC-funded talent wars inflate it. **These do not apply to a trailer dealer.** Shown here only so the gap is explicit:

| Segment | Base | OTE | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SMB Account Executive | $70k | $135k | [RepVue](https://www.repvue.com/salaries/account-executive) | 2026-06 |
| Account Executive (all) | $100k | $200k | [RepVue](https://www.repvue.com/salaries/account-executive) (34,811 submissions) | 2026-06 |
| SaaS AE | $92k–$218k | ~$190k median | [Bridge Group 2024 SaaS AE Benchmark](https://blog.bridgegroupinc.com/2024-ae-metrics-compensation-benchmark) | 2024 |

> ⚠️ **These overstate a trailer-dealer closer by roughly 2–3×.** Even RepVue's *lowest* closing segment ($135k OTE) is pure tech. Anchor dollars to sections 1–2, not this table. See [`METHODOLOGY.md`](../METHODOLOGY.md) for the full SaaS-skew explanation.

## 4. Dealer-floor reality check (powersports / RV proxies)

Trailer-floor-closer pay isn't published cleanly, so the nearest real-world analogs — powersports and RV floor sales (similar $3k–$30k unit band, retail-floor model) — serve as a cross-check:

- **Powersports floor sales:** avg **$56,329**, with $35k (25th) – $65k (75th), top ~$80k (90th). [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Salaries/Powersports-Sales-Salary)
- **RV sales (floor):** avg **~$57,617**; Camping World "RV Sales Associate" ~$87,917. [RV Rank](https://www.rvrank.com/post/average-commission-sales/) / [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Camping-World-RV-Sales-Associate-Salaries-E19691_D_KO14,32.htm)
- **Auto (high-volume proxy):** entry $35k–$45k, experienced $50k–$80k, top performers >$100k. [AutoFinder](https://autofinder.com/insights/how-much-commission-does-a-car-salesman-make)

> ⚠️ The one trailer-*titled* figure — Glassdoor "Trailer Sales Representative" ~$121k — is **contaminated** by semi-trailer / commercial-fleet B2B reps and OEM territory managers and should **not** be used for a utility/cargo-trailer floor closer.

## 5. Synthesis — recommended base + OTE

All streams converge on **~$55k–$75k total cash for a solid closer**, ~$60k–$67k median (national), ~$63k (Lancaster), with top performers at $90k–$130k. Translating that into a pay plan:

| Tier | Base / draw | Target OTE (national) | Target OTE (Lancaster, ×~0.94) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry / ramp** | $36k–$40k draw | ~$50k | ~$47k |
| **Solid closer (target)** | **$40k–$45k** | **~$67k** | **~$63k** |
| **Strong performer** | (same base) | ~$95k | ~$88k |
| **Top performer (design ceiling)** | (same base) | $130k+ | $120k+ |

Base sits at ~60% of target OTE (see [`pay-mix.md`](pay-mix.md)); the variable half comes from gross-margin commission (see [`commission.md`](commission.md)); the Lancaster discount is applied per [`regional.md`](regional.md).
